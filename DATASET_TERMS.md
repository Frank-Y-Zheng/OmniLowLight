# OmniLowLight dataset access terms

Version 1.0 · 15 October 2026

OmniLowLight is made available by reviewed request for **non-commercial academic research** at universities and research institutions.

By requesting and using the dataset, applicants agree to the following:

1. **Approved research use.** Use the supplied materials only for the academic research purpose described in the approved request. Commercial use, resale, and commercial exploitation are not authorized.
2. **No redistribution.** Do not upload, publish, sell, share, or redistribute dataset images, annotations, archived model outputs, or copies of those materials. Do not forward download links, passwords, or access credentials. Other users must request access separately.
3. **Privacy.** Do not attempt to identify people depicted in the images or use the data for surveillance, harassment, or other privacy-invasive purposes.
4. **Research attribution.** Cite the OmniLowLight paper, identify the dataset version used, and document preprocessing and transformations when reporting results.
5. **Request review.** Provide accurate affiliation and research information using your own institutional email. Submitting a request is not approval; access instructions are supplied privately after review.

Aggregate numerical results may be reported with appropriate citation. Permission to publish dataset images or release derived image collections must be obtained separately from the maintainers. This access agreement does not transfer ownership or grant an unrestricted redistribution license.

## Request information

The access form collects your name, institutional email, affiliation, role or research group, research purpose, requested materials, and agreement to these terms. The maintainers use responses to assess requests and manage access. Responses are not published in this repository or shown to other applicants. Google processes the form according to its own applicable terms and privacy policy.
